# Frontend App — Surface Brief

## Scope & Mode

Operate surface: the research-graph workspace shell — top bar, interactive canvas, and right-hand Agent Team / Live Activity Feed sidebar.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A research tool that reads like a scientist's bound lab notebook — warm paper, ink, and serif annotation — rather than a dark "terminal dashboard". The interface refuses the dark-mode dev-tool cliché so the working graph feels like a desk, not a server room.

OWN-WORLD: Warm-paper ground (#F7F5F0) under white cards with hairline #E5E0D8 borders; charcoal ink text (#1C1917) with #78716C muted; ink-black and warm amber (#B45309) as the two accents. Newsreader serif for the masthead and section headings, Inter for interface body, JetBrains Mono for metrics, ratings, and statuses. Thin 6px scrollbar and a lucide icon system in one stroke weight.

STORY: The researcher opens the workspace and immediately reads it as their notebook — serif masthead, paper canvas, a graph of papers/questions/notes. They add sources via ghost buttons, run analysis (ink) and reproduction (amber), and supervise the agent feed — approving with ink, rejecting with a struck red — until the trail is complete and exportable.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-height three-region shell. Top: paper masthead with serif "ResearchGraph", four ghost add/export buttons, and three right-aligned AI actions (ink Analyze, amber Reproduction, amber-outline What If). Center: the warm-paper graph canvas. Right: a 320px white sidebar with the Agent Team roster (mono ratings) and the Live Activity Feed with crisp Approve/Reject controls.

FORM: seed: none (pinned) — "Editorial Lab Notebook (Warm Paper / Light Mode)". The direction is pinned whole by the brief, so no roll or decision page ran.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
